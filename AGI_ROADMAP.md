# 🧠 AGI Roadmap: From Cognitive AI to Human-Level Intelligence

## 📊 Current State: Advanced Cognitive AI Assistant

The Arena AI Agent is a **sophisticated cognitive AI assistant** with:
- ✅ 50+ cognitive modules
- ✅ Belief system with time decay and provenance
- ✅ Goal interpretation and verification
- ✅ Memory and learning with lesson extraction
- ✅ 30+ specialized tools
- ✅ Voice pipeline with wake word
- ✅ 876 tests passing (100% pass rate)
- ✅ Production-ready code quality

**However, this is NOT AGI (Artificial General Intelligence).**

---

## 🎯 What is AGI?

**AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)** is AI that:
- ✅ Can understand, learn, and apply knowledge across **any domain**
- ✅ Has **human-level reasoning** and problem-solving
- ✅ Can **set its own goals** autonomously
- ✅ Has **self-awareness** and consciousness
- ✅ Can **transfer learning** across completely different domains
- ✅ Has **common sense reasoning**
- ✅ Can **learn continuously** from environment
- ✅ Has **emotional intelligence**
- ✅ Can **create and innovate** autonomously
- ✅ Has **ethical reasoning**
- ✅ Can **make autonomous decisions** without human guidance
