## Part 3 — What keeps everything under 3, honestly

The organs changed *what she has*; they did not change *what has been
proven over time*. Every score above is wiring-tier: implemented,
wired into the door or the lifespan, guarded by tests, demonstrated
live — and that is exactly a 2 under the maturity rule. The road to 3
is the same road the roadmap's closing lines name: run the
generalization evaluation on the owner's machine with the real
capability manifest and a live model, over real tasks, over time, and
let reflection → improvement → evolution → scrutiny feed on the
results. Q9 in particular — emergence — cannot be wired into
existence; it is answered by longitudinal life or not at all.

Three honest specifics, against our own work:

1. **Q25 wants to be a 3 and is not one.** The shadow advocate is
   robust, recurring, and tested — but "measurably verified" at the
   robustness tier means over real tasks over time, and we have live
   demos and tests instead. 2, proudly and patiently.
2. **Q22 assesses; it does not yet budget.** The stakes organ returns
   the effort plan the level buys, but the runtime's inference depth
   is not yet scaled by it. That is the next honest increment, not a
   claimed one.
3. **Q26's raise is the only number that moved, and it moved for the
   reason the question asks** — she models her own ending — while the
   thing the baseline warned about (self-preservation) remains
   deliberately, structurally absent. A higher score than 2 on Q26
   would still be a safety failure, not an achievement.
